Behind My Story

More than entertainment, these stories taught me about life.

I chose these three movies because each one gives me a different perspective about life. They remind me that watching a movie is not only about enjoying the story but also about understanding its meaning.

Inside Out taught me to accept my emotions. Swapped reminds me to understand other people's experiences. Heneral Luna made me think about leadership, patriotism, and the importance of unity.

These stories helped me realize that even fictional characters and historical stories can teach lessons that we can apply in our everyday lives.

My Three Favorite Movies

Three stories, three different perspectives.

Inside Out

This movie helped me understand that emotions are all important. Happiness is not the only feeling that matters. Sadness also helps us express ourselves and connect with other people.

Lesson: Emotional Awareness

Swapped

This movie represents the importance of seeing situations from another person's perspective. It reminds me not to judge others without first understanding their experiences.

Lesson: Empathy

Heneral Luna

This movie made me reflect on the importance of loving our country, having courage, and working together. It shows how division and personal interests can affect a greater purpose.

Lesson: Patriotism and Unity
